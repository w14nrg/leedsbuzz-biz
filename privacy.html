Privacy Policy

LeedsBuzz.biz is an independent Leeds supporter website. This policy explains what information the site uses and why.

Information you provide

When you create an account, submit a rating, save a team or contact the site, LeedsBuzz.biz may store the information needed to provide that feature. Do not submit sensitive personal information through public site features.

Technical information

The service may process basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and security logs. This is used to operate, protect and improve the service.

Cookies and local storage

LeedsBuzz.biz may use browser storage to keep you signed in, remember choices, save teams and provide account features. Private administration tokens should only be remembered on trusted devices.

X and external services

The site may display or link to public posts and source material from X and other football sources. Using those external services is subject to their own privacy terms. LeedsBuzz.biz does not sell X data or user data.

AI features

BizBot may send a user’s question and limited conversation context to an AI service to generate an answer. Users should not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in BizBot messages.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the service, legal obligations and security. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to legal and security requirements.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent through the contact method published on LeedsBuzz.biz.

Independent site

LeedsBuzz.biz is an independent supporter project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Leeds United Football Club.
