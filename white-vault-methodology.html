WHAT COUNTS

Competitive Leeds United first-team records

Appearances

Competitive first-team appearances for Leeds United. Friendlies are not included in the historical career-table totals.

Starts and substitutes

Shown only when the source table supplies the split. The two figures are checked against the total where possible.

Goals

Competitive Leeds United goals. Goalkeeper clean sheets and modern advanced data are kept in their own fields rather than being inferred.

Career span

The first and final calendar years shown in the published Leeds United career record. “Present” is used only for a player in the current squad snapshot.

PROFILE LABELS

What the coverage badge means

  • Source Enriched: most core fields, an extended story and attached sources are available.
  • Verified Core: the main career record is present but some supporting fields remain incomplete.
  • Core Record: the player qualifies for the Vault, but the profile is awaiting more source-backed detail.
THREE DIFFERENT SCORES

Facts, ratings and votes do not mix

  • Career record: factual source-backed information.
  • White DNA: LeedsBuzz.biz’s position-aware football assessment, with its method and confidence shown.
  • Fan Rating: the average of signed-in supporter votes out of 100.
SOURCE LADDER

Where the archive looks first

Official Leeds United material is preferred for current squad status, club announcements and season reviews. The published Leeds United player career tables provide the launch-wide historical appearance, start, substitute and goal structure. Individual profile summaries may be used for context only when the page can be confidently matched to the Leeds United player.

CORRECTIONS

Accuracy before false completeness

An empty field is not a claim that an event never happened. When a player-specific honours list has not been verified, that field is left blank; it does not mean the player won no trophies. LeedsBuzz.biz will not manufacture a date, trophy, record, nationality, position or story merely to make a profile look full.

Source snapshots change, especially for active players. A later verified source can update a profile without changing its White Vault identity, fan votes, saved XIs or comparison links.
